# Bug: Pallet Becomes Stuck After Moving It

## Game
The Last of Us Part I

## Platform
PlayStation 5

## Category
Gameplay / Environmental Interaction

## Severity
Medium

## Description
A movable pallet can become stuck after being pulled into position, preventing it from being moved again.

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Reach the area with the electrified water.
2. Open the garage door.
3. Pull the pallet out.
4. Move the pallet into position near the door.
5. Attempt to move the pallet again.

## Expected Result
The player should be able to continue moving the pallet as needed.

## Actual Result
The pallet becomes stuck and can no longer be moved.
