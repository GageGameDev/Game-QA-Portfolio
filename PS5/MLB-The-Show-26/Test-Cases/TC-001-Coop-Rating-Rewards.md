TC-001 — Co-op Rating and Reward Verification

Game: MLB The Show 26

Platform: PlayStation 5

Test Case ID: TC-001

Feature: Online Co-op Rating and Reward System

Test Type: Functional / Regression Testing

Priority: High

Execution Status: Not Run

Test Objective

Verify that the online co-op rating and reward system accurately tracks player performance, displays consistent ratings, and distributes eligible rewards after completed matches.

This test case is designed to investigate potential inconsistencies in co-op ratings and reward distribution.

Preconditions

- MLB The Show 26 is installed and updated.
- PlayStation Network connectivity is available.
- Two players are available for online co-op.
- Both players can access the relevant co-op mode.
- Current player ratings and reward progress are recorded.
- Any applicable reward eligibility requirements are known.

Test Steps

Step| Test Action| Expected Result
1| Launch MLB The Show 26.| Game launches successfully.
2| Navigate to the online co-op mode.| Co-op interface loads correctly.
3| Record the current co-op rating for each player.| Ratings are visible and can be documented.
4| Review available rewards and their requirements.| Reward conditions are clearly displayed.
5| Invite another player to a co-op session.| Both players join successfully.
6| Start and complete a co-op match.| Match completes and results are recorded.
7| Review the postgame rating information.| Ratings update according to the game's rules and remain consistent between relevant displays.
8| Check earned rewards.| All rewards earned under the published requirements are granted correctly.
9| Return to the co-op menu.| Rating and reward information remains accurate.
10| Restart the game and recheck the information.| Previously recorded results and earned rewards persist correctly.

Expected Overall Result

The game should accurately track and display co-op ratings, award all eligible rewards, and preserve results between sessions.

Pass Criteria

The test passes when:

- Co-op ratings display consistently.
- Rating changes follow the game's stated rules.
- Eligible rewards are distributed correctly.
- Rating and reward progress persists after restarting.

Fail Criteria

The test fails if any verified discrepancy occurs, including:

- Inconsistent co-op ratings between relevant game screens.
- Incorrect rating changes compared with documented game rules.
- Eligible rewards missing after match completion.
- Rating or reward progress failing to persist.
