# Bug #002 - Defensive Player Partially Sinks Into Field During Pre-Game Presentation

## Game
MLB The Show 26

## Platform
PlayStation 5

## Mode
Ranked Co-op

## Category
Visual / Animation

## Severity
Low

## Priority
Low

## Description
During the pre-game presentation, a defensive player positioned near the pitcher can appear partially submerged beneath the field surface.

The player's lower body clips through the ground before returning to the correct position prior to the first pitch.

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Launch MLB The Show 26.
2. Enter a Ranked Co-op game.
3. Allow the pre-game presentation to play normally.
4. Observe the defensive players on the field before the first pitch.
5. Watch the player positioned near the pitcher.

## Expected Result
All player models should stand correctly on the field surface during the pre-game presentation.

## Actual Result
A defensive player's model appears partially below the field surface, making the player look as though they are sinking into the ground.

The positioning corrects itself before gameplay begins.

## Reproduction Rate
Observed more than 10 times during testing.

Estimated reproduction during the original testing period: approximately 10/10 observations.

## Notes
The issue appears to be cosmetic and does not appear to affect gameplay.

It may be related to player positioning, animation placement, or collision with the field surface during the presentation sequence.

## Evidence
Screenshot or video showing the player model partially beneath the field surface.
