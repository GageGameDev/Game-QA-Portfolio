Bug Report 001 — Super Suction TV Obstruction

Game: Luigi's Mansion 3
Platform: Nintendo Switch
Location: Floor 4 — The Great Stage, Auditorium
Category: Environmental / Object Interaction
Severity: Medium
Status: Workaround Confirmed — Further Testing Needed

Bug Description

After unlocking Super Suction and returning to Floor 4, a wooden platform remained positioned in front of a television required to access a hidden balcony containing a collectible gem.

Despite using Super Suction, the wooden structure could not be removed, obstructing access to the television.

Steps to Reproduce

1. Unlock Super Suction through normal story progression.
2. Return to Floor 4 and enter the Auditorium.
3. Connect the Poltergust G-00 to the electrical socket.
4. Activate Super Suction and remove the back wall of the stage.
5. Approach the television behind the stage.
6. Attempt to remove the obstructing wooden platform using Super Suction.

Expected Result

The environment should allow the player to access the television and reach the hidden balcony without needing to reload the floor.

Actual Result

The wooden platform remained in front of the television and did not respond to Super Suction.

Confirmed Workaround

Leaving Floor 4 using the elevator and then returning to the Auditorium resolved the obstruction, allowing the player to access the television.

Reproduction Status

Observed during one gameplay session. Further testing is necessary to determine whether the issue consistently occurs.

Evidence

- Screenshot captured showing the wooden structure obstructing the television.
- Screenshot to be added to this report.

QA Notes

The successful workaround suggests a possible environmental object-state or loading issue. The exact cause has not been confirmed.

Final Status: Resolved through floor reload; underlying issue not verified as permanently fixed.
