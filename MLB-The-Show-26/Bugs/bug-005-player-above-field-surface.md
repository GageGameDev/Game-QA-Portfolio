# Bug #005 - Player Model Appears Above Field Surface

## Game
MLB The Show 26

## Platform
PlayStation 5 - Retest Recommended

## Mode
Needs Retest

## Category
Visual / Player Positioning

## Severity
Low

## Priority
Low

## Description
A player model can appear positioned above the field instead of making proper contact with the playing surface.

The player appears to float or sit above the ground rather than being correctly aligned with the field.

## Steps to Reproduce
The exact trigger has not yet been isolated.

1. Launch MLB The Show 26.
2. Enter gameplay.
3. Observe player positioning during gameplay and presentation sequences.
4. Watch for players whose feet or body are positioned above the field surface.
5. Record the game mode, stadium, player, animation, and situation when the issue occurs.

## Expected Result
Player models should remain correctly aligned with the field surface during all gameplay and presentation animations.

## Actual Result
A player model appears elevated above the ground instead of correctly contacting the field surface.
